# Artwork provenance

## Active icon: Flow Steward publisher mark

This independent Flow Steward extension uses its publisher's neutral mark. It is
not the Meta, Facebook or Instagram logo and does not assert affiliation,
sponsorship, endorsement or partner status. ROOT explicitly requested this
publisher-owned fallback when provider artwork usage could not be cleared.
This is the task-specific exception to the default branded-icon rule; the mark
must never be presented as official Meta artwork.

- Transformation: none; copied byte-for-byte from the pinned publisher source.
- Ownership: Flow Steward publisher-owned artwork, used to identify this

On 2026-11-16 the pinned original and final file were verified byte-identical.
The exact PNG was visually inspected at 32, 64 and 128 pixels on white and
#111827 dark card backgrounds: full mark visible, no clipping or distortion,

This is unpublished card-size preparation evidence, not a claim that a public

The same exact icon and this provenance must be checked in both native ZIPs

## Why Meta corporate artwork is not active

The old corporate logo was sourced from the Meta newsroom, with original bytes
and prior provenance retained only in the release owner's local evidence.
It is not shipped in the current source tree or native runtime ZIPs.

Current official guidance, reviewed 2026-11-16:
https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/ (updated June 2026).
It states that all use of the Meta logo requires approval through a Meta
counterpart and Brand Review, and that the corporate logo should not represent
Facebook. No such approval was supplied. The old about.meta.com URL requires
login; the www.meta.com guidance is publicly readable. No contact, permission
request or acceptance on behalf of the publisher was submitted.

Names Meta, Facebook and Instagram identify the external services supported by
this extension. Their marks belong to their respective owners. Live provider
OAuth and advertising acceptance are separate from this artwork review.
